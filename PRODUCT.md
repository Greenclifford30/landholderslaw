# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Chicago-area and Illinois homeowners, buyers, sellers, property investors, and businesses seeking clear legal guidance for high-stakes property decisions.

## Product Purpose

Landholders Law provides direct, attorney-led counsel for real-estate matters, land trusts, and estate planning. The public site should help a prospective client understand the practice, establish trust in Ted M. Word, and request a consultation or call the firm.

## Positioning

Personal property counsel: clients work with an experienced attorney who makes complex property decisions understandable and helps them identify a clear next step.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients often arrive while buying, selling, financing, protecting, disputing, or planning the transfer of property. The primary public journey is from evaluating the firm to requesting a consultation. Real Estate is the lead service; Land Trusts and Estate Planning remain visible secondary services.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public routes in this redesign are the homepage and attorney profile.
- The consultation workflow collects name, telephone, email, and requested service and forwards those fields through the existing AWS-backed API route.
- The existing `/admin` and `/client` prototypes remain reachable but are outside the redesign and must not be presented as production portals.
- The practice serves Chicago-area clients and clients elsewhere in Illinois.
- No dedicated service pages, scheduling integration, portal authentication, analytics, testimonials, case results, pricing, awards, or deployment work are included.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the Landholders Law name and Ted M. Word, Esq. as the responsible attorney.
- Evolve, rather than discard, the recognizable home-in-hands mark and black/gold identity.
- Preserve Ted's motto: “Attorney by training, Husband by choice, Father by calling, and Gentleman by God!”
- The experience should feel personal, authoritative, warm, and accessible. Avoid generic corporate-law conventions, anonymous courthouse imagery, and interchangeable navy-and-gold branding.

## Product Principles

1. Put direct access to an experienced attorney ahead of institutional posturing.
2. Make property-law choices feel understandable without oversimplifying their importance.
3. Earn trust with verified credentials, useful specificity, and clear next steps.
4. Respect prospective-client privacy and never imply that a form submission creates representation.
5. Keep the public experience focused on consultation conversion while clearly distinguishing unfinished internal prototypes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. The experience must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, high contrast, readable type, clear form errors, and responsive layouts from small mobile screens through desktop.
